Current Research

Heatwaves · Vulnerability · Open-source GeoAI

Spatiotemporal dynamics, drivers and population vulnerability of heatwaves across Nigeria's climate zones and states (2003 to 2025)

Nigeria has no national account of how its heat hazard is changing. Most studies cover a few cities over short records and reduce heat to days above a fixed threshold. This project separates heatwave frequency, duration and intensity using the Excess Heat Factor on ERA5-Land, reads every result through two lenses at once (the six climate zones and the 36 states plus the FCT), explains the drivers with gradient boosting and SHAP, and projects exposure to 2050 under SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5 with NEX-GDDP-CMIP6.

The analysis is packaged as a four-tier open-source system, from automated Earth observation ingestion to an agentic natural language interface, so that Nigerian climate and health agencies can run it without licensed software.

  • Surface Urban Heat Island of Ibadan

    Research

    Landsat-based study of Ibadan's surface urban heat island (2025). The urban core averaged 39.8 °C against 34.7 °C for the rural ring, a SUHI intensity of 5.0 °C. LST and NDVI were strongly negatively correlated (r = -0.93), and significant Getis-Ord Gi* heat clustering covered 25.1 percent of the study area. Manuscript under review with Discover Geoscience.

    View code on GitHub

  • Trona Geochemistry, Lake Chad

    Research

    Brine evolution and detrital provenance of trona from the northeastern perimeter of Lake Chad, Republic of Chad, using petrography, quantitative XRD and ICP-OES/ICP-MS geochemistry. Trona makes up 51.3 to 79.4 wt% of the material, no sulfate phase occurs, and one principal component explains 84.2 percent of compositional variance. The rare earth budget sits entirely in the silicate fraction. Manuscript under review with Discover Geoscience.
